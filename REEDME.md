## 🧬 The Architecture of My Mind

I thrive in the ambiguous parts of development. I don't just want to know *how* to build it; I want to know *why* it works under the hood.

> **My Workflow:** Idea ➔ Architecture ➔ Frontend/Backend ➔ Database ➔ AI/Integrations ➔ Desktop/Mobile ➔ Deployment ➔ **Real Users.**

- ⚙️ **Root Cause Engineering:** Diving deep into algorithmic efficiency, native dependencies, and OS-level quirks.
- 📂 **Binary & Filesystem:** Reverse-engineering OTF/TTF fonts, parsing SFNT tables, and manipulating encrypted archives.
- 🌍 **Production-Grade DevOps:** Handling real-world deployment for platforms serving thousands of active users.
